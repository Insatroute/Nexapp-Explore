The SD-WAN fabric — the overlay you design and deploy, as opposed to the physical estate under Network. A row is one topology: its type, the overlay subnet it uses, how many hubs and spokes it has, which features are on, and whether it is up.

## Why it sits here

The tunnels laid over the network, kept apart from the physical estate in Network — the fabric is the thing you design, the devices are what carry it.
