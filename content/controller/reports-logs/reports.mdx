The report catalogue: what each report covers, the category it belongs to, and when it was last updated. Reports are opened from here, and a custom report can be built with the customize wizard — choosing the columns and the devices it covers.

## Why it sits here

What the system produced and what it recorded. Reports are generated on demand; logs are the running record — different jobs, but both are "look at what happened", so they share a section rather than being scattered across Intelligence and Administration.

<Callout type="info">Authored note, from the `NAV` array in `Sidebar.jsx`.</Callout>

## What you can do here

### Actions

- Create custom report
- Delete custom report
- Edit custom report

### Reading and lookups

- Browse and search custom reports
- Browse and search report devices
- Browse and search report templates
- View report columns

**Opens:** `CustomizeWizard` — some actions above are performed in it.
