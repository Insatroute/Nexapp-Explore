The report catalogue: what each report covers, the category it belongs to, and when it was last updated. Reports are opened from here, and a custom report can be built with the customize wizard — choosing the columns and the devices it covers.

## Why it sits here

What the system produced and what it recorded. Reports are generated on demand; logs are the running record — different jobs, but both are "look at what happened", so they share a section rather than being scattered across Intelligence and Administration.

<Callout type="info">Authored note, from the `NAV` array in `Sidebar.jsx`.</Callout>

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Create custom report** — Builds one in the customize wizard — pick the columns and the devices it covers.
- **Delete custom report** — Removes it. Built-in templates cannot be deleted; only custom ones.
- **Edit custom report** — Changes a custom report's columns or device scope.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search custom reports** — Reports built here rather than shipped, from `/reports/custom/`.
- **Browse and search report devices** — The devices a report can be scoped to, for the same wizard.
- **Browse and search report templates** — The built-in reports, from `/reports/template/` — the catalogue this page lists.
- **View report columns** — The columns available for a given report, which is what the wizard offers as choices.

**Opens:** `CustomizeWizard` — some actions above are performed in it.
