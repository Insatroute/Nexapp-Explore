Hosts the DPI engine has seen behind a router but that were never registered — hostname, MAC, address, how much they moved, and when they were first and last seen.

Read-only: these rows are produced by the DPI engine.

<Callout type="info">Served by the shared `AppIntelHub` component via the `/app-intelligence/:tab` route. Facts below describe that component, which renders several tabs.</Callout>

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Delete resource** — Removes a discovered device via `DELETE /api/v1/dpi/devices/<id>/`.
- **Edit resource** — Creates and edits a discovered device through the shared resource form. Read-only in practice — these records are written by the engine, not by hand.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search resource** — Lists discovered devices from `GET /api/v1/dpi/devices/`, with columns Device, Hostname, MAC address, IP address, Status, Total download, Total upload, Type, First seen, Last seen.

**Also calls:** `appDetail` — listed verbatim because the name does not match a known verb.

**Opens:** `AppDatabase` · `ResourceTable` — some actions above are performed in these.
