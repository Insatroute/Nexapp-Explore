What the DPI engine saw, per application: bytes down and up, flow count, and the device and window it was observed in. A read-only record rather than something you configure.

Read-only: these rows are produced by the DPI engine.

<Callout type="info">Served by the shared `AppIntelHub` component via the `/app-intelligence/:tab` route. Facts below describe that component, which renders several tabs.</Callout>

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Delete resource** — Removes a traffic record via `DELETE /api/v1/dpi/traffic/<id>/`.
- **Edit resource** — Creates and edits a traffic record through the shared resource form. Read-only in practice — these records are written by the engine, not by hand.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search resource** — Lists traffic records from `GET /api/v1/dpi/traffic/`, with columns Application, Category, Protocol, Download, Upload, Total, Flows, Device, From, To.

**Also calls:** `appDetail` — listed verbatim because the name does not match a known verb.

**Opens:** `AppDatabase` · `ResourceTable` — some actions above are performed in these.
