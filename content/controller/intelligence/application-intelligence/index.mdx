A disclosure rather than a link: the DPI analytics have four read-only tables (Applications, App Traffic, Discovered Devices, Snapshots) and no landing page of their own — the same shape as Monitoring below.

`Application Intelligence` is a disclosure in the sidebar — it opens, it does not navigate. It covers:

- [Applications](/controller/intelligence/application-intelligence/applications) — The applications the DPI engine recognises.
- [App Traffic](/controller/intelligence/application-intelligence/app-traffic) — What the DPI engine saw, per application: bytes down and up, flow count, and the device and window it was observed in.
- [Discovered Devices](/controller/intelligence/application-intelligence/discovered-devices) — Hosts the DPI engine has seen behind a router but that were never registered — hostname, MAC, address, how much they moved, and when they were first and last seen.
- [Snapshots](/controller/intelligence/application-intelligence/snapshots) — Point-in-time health of the DPI engine on a device: how many flows it was tracking, how many it could identify, and what it was costing in memory and CPU.

<Callout type="info">The note above is authored, from the `NAV` array in `Sidebar.jsx`.</Callout>
