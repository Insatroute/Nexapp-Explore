Point-in-time health of the DPI engine on a device: how many flows it was tracking, how many it could identify, and what it was costing in memory and CPU.

Read-only: these rows are produced by the DPI engine.

<Callout type="info">Served by the shared `AppIntelHub` component via the `/app-intelligence/:tab` route. Facts below describe that component, which renders several tabs.</Callout>

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Delete resource** — Removes a snapshot via `DELETE /api/v1/dpi/snapshots/<id>/`.
- **Edit resource** — Creates and edits a snapshot through the shared resource form. Read-only in practice — these records are written by the engine, not by hand.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search resource** — Lists snapshots from `GET /api/v1/dpi/snapshots/`, with columns Device, Flow count, Identified, Unknown, Devices, Memory, Uptime, CPU, Timestamp.

**Also calls:** `appDetail` — listed verbatim because the name does not match a known verb.

**Opens:** `AppDatabase` · `ResourceTable` — some actions above are performed in these.
