The TACACS+ servers this controller runs: what they listen on, whether authentication and accounting are enabled, the deploy mode, and whether the running configuration matches what has been asked for — a pending count means changes are staged but not yet reloaded.

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Apply or push TACACS+ server** — Pushes the staged configuration and reloads the server, which is what clears Pending and updates Last reload.
- **Edit TACACS+ server** — Changes a server's settings. The change is staged rather than live — the **Pending** count is what tells you a reload is owed.
- **Refresh TACACS+ server** — Re-reads the server's current state rather than waiting for the page to poll — the way to confirm a reload actually took.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search TACACS+ servers** — The TACACS+ servers this controller runs, from `tacacs/server/` — what they listen on, whether authentication and accounting are on, and whether the running config matches what has been asked for.

**Opens:** `TacacsServerDrawer` — some actions above are performed in it.
