Bulk user creation. A batch generates or imports a set of RADIUS users in one operation — the strategy says which — and records how many users it produced, when the credentials expire, and lets the generated credentials be retrieved.

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Create RADIUS batch** — Generates or imports a set of users in one operation. The strategy decides which; the credentials it produces are retrievable from the row afterwards, until they expire.
- **Delete RADIUS batch** — Removes the batch record. Check what it created before deleting — the record is the only place the generated credentials are listed.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search organizations** — The organization list — what the scope picker narrows the page by, and what fills the Organization field on its forms. Almost every record in the controller is organization-scoped, which is why this call appears on so many pages.
- **Browse and search RADIUS batches** — Past bulk-creation runs, from `radius-admin/batch/` — the strategy used, how many users it produced, and when the generated credentials expire.

**Opens:** `RadiusBatchDrawer` — some actions above are performed in it.
