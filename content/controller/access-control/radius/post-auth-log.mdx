The authentication log: every attempt and whether it succeeded, with the username, the client and NAS MAC addresses and when it happened. This is where a failing login is diagnosed.

## What you can do here

- **Browse and search organizations** — The organization list — what the scope picker narrows the page by, and what fills the Organization field on its forms. Almost every record in the controller is organization-scoped, which is why this call appears on so many pages.
- **Browse and search RADIUS post authentication** — Every authentication attempt FreeRADIUS handled and what it answered — accept or reject, per username, with the station identifiers. Read-only and enforced as such: FreeRADIUS writes this table and nothing in the console does.
