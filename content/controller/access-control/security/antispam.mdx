Mail filtering, pointed at a relay host with optional SASL authentication.

Scoped like the rest of Security: fleet-wide, or narrowed to an organization or a single device.

<Callout type="info">Served by the shared `SecurityHub` component via the `/security/:tab` route. Facts below describe that component, which renders several tabs.</Callout>

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Delete resource** — Removes a policy via `DELETE /api/v1/security/antispam/<id>/`.
- **Edit resource** — Creates and edits a policy through the shared resource form. Fields: Service, Relay host, Port, SASL username.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search resource** — Lists policies from `GET /api/v1/security/antispam/`, with columns Name, Service, Relay host, State.

**Opens:** `ResourceTable` — some actions above are performed in it.
