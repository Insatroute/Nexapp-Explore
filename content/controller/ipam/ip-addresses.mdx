Individual addresses, and the day-to-day half of IPAM: allocating one, or hunting a free one. Each row shows the address, the subnet it belongs to and the organization that owns it.

Addresses come first in this section because subnets are the setup step you visit far less often.

## Why it sits here

Its own section rather than a disclosure inside Network. Addressing is a subject an operator works IN — allocating a subnet, hunting a free address — not one entry among the devices and links it describes, and filed under Network it cost two clicks to reach from anywhere else. Addresses first: it is the day-to-day view, and subnets are the setup step you visit far less often.

<Callout type="info">Authored note, from the `NAV` array in `Sidebar.jsx`.</Callout>

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Delete IP** — Releases an address back to its subnet (`DELETE ipam/ip-address/<id>/`). Nothing on a device changes — IPAM records the allocation, it does not configure it.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search all IPS** — Every allocated address, from `ipam/ip-address/`, with the subnet and organization it belongs to.
- **Browse and search organizations** — The organization list — what the scope picker narrows the page by, and what fills the Organization field on its forms. Almost every record in the controller is organization-scoped, which is why this call appears on so many pages.
- **Browse and search subnets** — The subnet list the filter narrows by, and the subnet field when allocating an address.
