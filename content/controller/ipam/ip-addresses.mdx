Individual addresses, and the day-to-day half of IPAM: allocating one, or hunting a free one. Each row shows the address, the subnet it belongs to and the organization that owns it.

Addresses come first in this section because subnets are the setup step you visit far less often.

## Why it sits here

Its own section rather than a disclosure inside Network. Addressing is a subject an operator works IN — allocating a subnet, hunting a free address — not one entry among the devices and links it describes, and filed under Network it cost two clicks to reach from anywhere else. Addresses first: it is the day-to-day view, and subnets are the setup step you visit far less often.
