What the network REPORTS about itself, rather than what you designed — collected by a parser and published as a graph. A row shows the parsing strategy, the format, how many nodes and links were found, and whether it is published.

Kept at top level rather than under Overlay Networks because the two are opposites: SD-WAN Fabric is the thing you deploy, this is the thing that comes back.

## Why it sits here

Top level, not under Overlay Networks — the fabric is what you DESIGN and deploy, this is what the network REPORTS back via a parser, and sitting them under one heading read as one feature. A SECTION, not a `section: null` group holding a disclosure. That distinction is load-bearing in the rail: a section renders as one icon that opens the flyout (see NavSection's `{!railed && ...}`), whereas a top-level disclosure renders inline and stays expandable — in a 58px column that means a stray caret and, on click, three rows whose labels the rail hides. The vendored app still has no landing page, so the heading is a disclosure, not a link, exactly as it was before.

<Callout type="info">Authored note, from the `NAV` array in `Sidebar.jsx`.</Callout>

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Create net topology** — Defines a topology for the parser to populate: the strategy that collects it, the format it arrives in, and the organization it belongs to. Nodes and links are then discovered into it rather than drawn by hand.
- **Delete net topology** — Removes the topology and, with it, the nodes and links collected under it.
- **Edit net topology** — Changes a topology's strategy, format or published state (`PATCH /network-topology/topology/<id>/?include_unpublished=true` — the flag is what lets an unpublished one be edited at all).

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search net topologies** — The parsed topologies a record here can belong to, so the form can offer a choice rather than an id.
- **Browse and search organizations** — The organization list — what the scope picker narrows the page by, and what fills the Organization field on its forms. Almost every record in the controller is organization-scoped, which is why this call appears on so many pages.

**Also calls:** `netTopologyFieldChoices` — listed verbatim because the name does not match a known verb.

**Opens:** `TopologyDrawer` — some actions above are performed in it.
