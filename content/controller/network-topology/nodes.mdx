The nodes a parsed topology found — each with the addresses it is known by and the topology it belongs to.

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Create net node** — Adds a node by hand (`POST /network-topology/node/`). Normally the parser discovers these — adding one manually is for something the collection strategy cannot see.
- **Delete net node** — Removes a node. Links that joined it are left pointing at something that no longer exists, so check the Links page after.
- **Edit net node** — Changes a node's label or the addresses it is known by. Addresses are what links are matched on, so editing them can change which links resolve.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search net nodes** — The nodes a link can join, for the same reason.
- **Browse and search net topologies** — The parsed topologies a record here can belong to, so the form can offer a choice rather than an id.
- **Browse and search organizations** — The organization list — what the scope picker narrows the page by, and what fills the Organization field on its forms. Almost every record in the controller is organization-scoped, which is why this call appears on so many pages.

**Opens:** `NodeDrawer` · `useNetTopologies` — some actions above are performed in these.
