Which hardware is permitted to join which organization. A row is a serial number, the organization it may join, whether it is admitted automatically, and who added it.

It is only consulted for organizations with "Require serial admission" switched on — a setting on the organization itself. Filed under Users & Organizations rather than Devices because the row is about entitlement, not about a device that already exists.

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Create allowed serial** — Permits a piece of hardware to join an organization. Only consulted when that organization has "Require serial admission" switched on — without it, the list is inert.
- **Delete allowed serial** — Withdraws permission. A device already registered stays registered — this governs joining, not membership.
- **Edit allowed serial** — Changes the organization, the auto-admit flag or the notes on an entry.
- **Import or upload allowed serials csv** — Loads a batch of serial numbers from a CSV instead of adding them one at a time.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search allowed serials** — The serial numbers permitted to join, from `serial-admission/allowed-serial/`, with the organization each may join and who added it.
- **Browse and search organizations** — The organization list — what the scope picker narrows the page by, and what fills the Organization field on its forms. Almost every record in the controller is organization-scoped, which is why this call appears on so many pages.

**Opens:** `AllowedSerialDrawer` · `SerialCsvDrawer` — some actions above are performed in these.
