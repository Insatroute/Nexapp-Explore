Who the tenants are and what they are entitled to — as opposed to Access Control further down, which is how a device or a person authenticates. The two were adjacent entries with the same icon, which read as one thing split in half. They are one thing: who exists, and who they belong to. Allowed serial numbers belongs with them rather than under Devices: the row says which ORGANIZATION a piece of hardware may join, and it is only consulted for orgs with "Require serial admission" on — a setting that lives on the organization form two entries above.

`Users & Organizations` is a disclosure in the sidebar — it opens, it does not navigate. It covers:

- [Users](/controller/administration/users-organizations/users) — Who can sign in, and what they are.
- [Organizations](/controller/administration/users-organizations/organizations) — The tenants.
- [Allowed Serial Numbers](/controller/administration/users-organizations/allowed-serial-numbers) — Which hardware is permitted to join which organization.
