The tenants. A row is one organization with its contact email, how many devices it owns and how many of those are online or offline right now — so tenant size and tenant health read together.

An organization is also the scope almost everything else in the console is filtered by.

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Delete organization** — Removes a tenant (`DELETE users/organization/<id>/`). Almost everything in the controller is organization-scoped, so read the Devices count on the row first: this is the widest-reaching delete in the console.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search organizations** — The organization list — what the scope picker narrows the page by, and what fills the Organization field on its forms. Almost every record in the controller is organization-scoped, which is why this call appears on so many pages.

**Also calls:** `useOrgDeviceCountQuery` — listed verbatim because the name does not match a known verb.
