Permission groups — named sets of permissions assigned to users. This is the `nexapp_users.Group` proxy the API enforces against, not stock `auth.group`, and it is a different model from Device Groups.

## Why it sits here

Two different models that used to share the word 'Groups'. This one is a named set of PERMISSIONS assigned to users; 'Device Groups' below is what devices belong to and what templates attach to. Both are labelled in full so neither reads as the general case of the other. Not 'Roles': that word is already spent on the user's Superuser/Staff/User tier (UserList.roleOf), and reusing it would trade one collision for a worse one.

<Callout type="info">Authored note, from the `NAV` array in `Sidebar.jsx`.</Callout>

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Delete group** — Removes a permission group. Users in it lose whatever it granted and fall back to their remaining groups, so check who is in it first.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search user groups** — The permission groups, from `users/group/` — the `nexapp_users.Group` proxy the API enforces against, not stock `auth.group`.
