The same structure the sidebar's scope picker carries — deployment → organizations → device groups → subgroups — drawn as an org chart, top-down, instead of as an indented tree.

It is a way of reading the shape of a deployment at a glance; the editing happens on the Organizations and Device Groups pages.

## What you can do here

- **Browse and search devices** — The device counts that hang off each branch of the chart — what makes the org chart show where the fleet actually sits, rather than just its shape.
- **Browse and search groups** — The device-group list — the second half of the scope picker, and the group field on this page's forms.
- **Browse and search organizations** — The organization list — what the scope picker narrows the page by, and what fills the Organization field on its forms. Almost every record in the controller is organization-scoped, which is why this call appears on so many pages.
