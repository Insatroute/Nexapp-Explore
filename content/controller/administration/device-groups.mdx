The groups devices belong to, and what those groups carry. A group has a parent, so the tree can go region → site → devices, and it can hold templates and variables that every device in it inherits.

Distinct from Permission Groups above, which are sets of permissions for users. These are what devices belong to and what templates attach to.

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Create device group** — Adds a group. A parent makes it part of a tree (region → site → devices), and templates attached here are inherited by every device in it.
- **Delete device group** — Removes a group. Its devices lose whatever it passed down, so look at the Templates and Variables columns before deleting.
- **Edit device group** — Changes a group's parent, templates or variables. Changing the templates changes the configuration of every device in the group.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search device groups** — The groups devices belong to, from `controller/group/` — each with its parent, and the templates and variables it passes down.
- **Browse and search groups** — The device-group list — the second half of the scope picker, and the group field on this page's forms.
- **Browse and search organizations** — The organization list — what the scope picker narrows the page by, and what fills the Organization field on its forms. Almost every record in the controller is organization-scoped, which is why this call appears on so many pages.
- **Browse and search templates** — The templates that can be attached to a group, for the group form.

**Opens:** `DeviceGroupDrawer` — some actions above are performed in it.
