The site's name and domain — two fields from django.contrib.sites, and neither is cosmetic. The domain is interpolated into the links in every alert email, so a wrong value here produces mail whose links go nowhere.

## Why it sits here

Mirrors the admin's Settings group (registered in sla_metrics/apps.py at position 470) so the two navs stay recognisable as the same product. Notifications and Email & Alerts are ported; the rest hand back to the admin rather than disappearing from the menu.

<Callout type="info">Authored note, from the `NAV` array in `Sidebar.jsx`.</Callout>
