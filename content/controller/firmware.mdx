Firmware in three parts: the CATEGORIES that group images, the BUILDS themselves with their version and OS identifier, and the UPGRADE batches that push a build to devices and report how far each one got.

A build belongs to a category and targets an OS identifier, which is what stops an image being offered to hardware it does not fit.

## Why it sits here

No heading. The hub carries builds, categories and mass upgrades as its own tabs, so "Services" was a section wrapping exactly one destination — an extra click and an extra word for nothing.
