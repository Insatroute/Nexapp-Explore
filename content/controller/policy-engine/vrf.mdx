Virtual routing and forwarding instances. VRF is the one exception to the no-device-picker rule on this page: its serializer requires a device outright, so a VRF is always bound to one.

Scope is the same across the Policy Engine: a policy with **Apply fleet-wide** set reaches every device, otherwise it is narrowed to an organization. There is deliberately no device picker — configuring a single router is what that router's own CPE page is for.

<Callout type="info">Served by the shared `PolicyEngine` component via the `/policy-engine/:tab` route. Facts below describe that component, which renders several tabs.</Callout>

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Delete resource** — Removes a policy via `DELETE /api/v1/nsbond/vrf-config/<id>/`.
- **Edit resource** — Creates and edits a policy through the shared resource form. Fields: VRF ID, Table ID, Device, Route distinguisher, RT import, RT export, Interfaces, Internet access.

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search resource** — Lists policies from `GET /api/v1/nsbond/vrf-config/`, with columns Name, VRF ID, RD, State.

**Opens:** `ResourceTable` — some actions above are performed in it.
