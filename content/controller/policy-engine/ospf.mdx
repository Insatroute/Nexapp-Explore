OSPF routing, including whether the router originates a default route and the BFD timers under it.

Scope is the same across the Policy Engine: a policy with **Apply fleet-wide** set reaches every device, otherwise it is narrowed to an organization. There is deliberately no device picker — configuring a single router is what that router's own CPE page is for.

<Callout type="info">Served by the shared `PolicyEngine` component via the `/policy-engine/:tab` route. Facts below describe that component, which renders several tabs.</Callout>

## What you can do here

### Actions

- **Delete resource** — Removes a policy via `DELETE /api/v1/nsbond/ospf-config/<id>/`.
- **Edit resource** — Creates and edits a policy through the shared resource form. Fields: Router ID, Redistribute connected/static/kernel, Default-information originate, Always advertise, Metric, and BFD (min RX/TX interval, detect multiplier).

### Reading and lookups

- **Browse and search resource** — Lists policies from `GET /api/v1/nsbond/ospf-config/`, with columns Name, Router ID, Scope, State.

**Opens:** `ResourceTable` — some actions above are performed in it.
