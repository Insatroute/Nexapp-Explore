High availability for the CONTROLLER'S OWN pair — not the routers'. It reports which node holds the VIP, which PostgreSQL node is primary, and whether the management plane is up, alongside a log of the events that got it there.

Failover actions are driven from this page, and their progress is polled while they run.

## What you can do here

- Browse and search dcdr events

**Also calls:** `useDcdrActionMutation` · `useDcdrActionProgressQuery` · `useDcdrStatusQuery` — listed verbatim because the name does not match a known verb.
