Templates and VPN servers are both "what gets pushed to a device", and were two unrelated-looking entries in a flat list.

`Configuration` is a disclosure in the sidebar — it opens, it does not navigate. It covers:

- [VPN Servers](/controller/network/configuration/vpn-servers) — The VPN servers devices connect back to.
- [Templates](/controller/network/configuration/templates) — Configuration templates — the units of config that get pushed to devices.

<Callout type="info">The note above is authored, from the `NAV` array in `Sidebar.jsx`.</Callout>
